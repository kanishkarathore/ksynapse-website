The MossPay Ecosystem

Unifying Fintech with Environmental Accountability. MossPay is a comprehensive dual-sided network engineered to reward consumers for sustainable lifestyle choices while providing business vendors with data-driven engines to map and mitigate carbon footprints.

Segment 01

For Consumers: The Sustainability First Payment App

✦ Carbon-Back Payments

Earn MossCoins for every eco-friendly purchase. Our unique algorithm calculates impact based on price, distance, and product sustainability.

✦ MossPay Wallet

Aggregate your digital coins to purchase carbon credits directly, making net-zero living an effortless reality.

✦ Discover Vendors

Find sustainable vendors nearby. Our marketplace connects you with businesses that align with your environmental values.

Segment 02

For Vendors: Smart POS & Carbon Engine

✦ Advanced POS System

A mobile-friendly, zero-cost billing and point-of-sale system provided on a Freemium model, lowering barriers to entry for sustainable MSMEs.

✦ Intelligent Inventory

Track the carbon footprint of your entire supply chain. Our AI-driven system helps discover eco-verified wholesalers and optimizes for lower emissions.

✦ Parent-Child Architecture

Our sophisticated system maps and verifies carbon credits throughout the supply chain, ensuring every "green" claim is backed by immutable data.

✦ Blockchain Powered

Ensuring data integrity and transparency for government schemes and international audits.

100%

Traceable Carbon Impact

From purchase to offset. Every transaction processed through the automated MossPay ledger maps a direct verification vector.
